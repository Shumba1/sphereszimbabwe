YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Spheres Zimbabwe is a developing Zimbabwean social and professional community. This notice explains how we collect and use information that people choose to share when registering as founding members.

What we collect

We ask for your name, WhatsApp number, location, main reason for joining and interests. You may choose to add an email address and age range. With separate, optional consent, we may also collect your relationship status, work situation and comfortable event budget. We collect the campaign link or referral code used to join, if one is present.

Why we use it

We use your details to administer community registrations, send membership and event invitations, understand the kinds of events members want and, with appropriate permission, suggest relevant gatherings or introductions. We do not publicly display your profile or provide another member with your contact details without your permission.

Optional personal details

Relationship status, work situation and event budget are optional and only collected if you actively consent to providing them. You can join without them and can ask us to stop using or delete them. We do not use these fields to exclude people on the basis of their financial circumstances or relationship status.

Messages and marketing

We may contact you through WhatsApp or email about your registration and Spheres community invitations. Partner offers and promotions are optional and are sent only when you separately opt in. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Where submissions are kept

Our launch form is processed and stored through Netlify Forms, a third-party service. Authorised Spheres administrators can access submissions in Netlify's dashboard, and Netlify processes the data as a service provider. We may securely export records for member administration. We do not sell member data.

How long we keep information

We keep member information while it is reasonably needed for community administration and invitations. We review inactive registrations and remove records no longer required. You can ask us to remove your information, subject to any legal obligations requiring retention.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete information about you, or withdraw optional consent, through the request form below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Contact us about privacy

Use the form below to contact the founding team about your data. Please do not send identity documents or sensitive additional information through this form.

Note: This launch-stage privacy notice should be reviewed and supplemented with the final operating entity's legal identity and contact details before public promotion.
