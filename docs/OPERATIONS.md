# Spheres Zimbabwe: first 50–100 members

This is the procedure for the free founding-member MVP. The owner must name the legal operator, approve retention periods and complete the privacy requirements before public collection. No payment, subscription, event booking or introduction service is active.

## Responsibility and access

The current Netlify team has one owner. Keep member records accessible only to the nominated operator. Enable two-factor authentication on that account. Do not share its password. If another operator is appointed, use an individual account and the least access the plan supports.

Both forms notify the existing Netlify account owner. Before launch, confirm actual receipt of one membership notification and one privacy-request notification, including spam folders. Check Forms directly each working day; an email is an alert, not the authoritative record.

## Review and acceptance

1. Review new `spheres-members` submissions and the spam folder. Require a non-blank name, plausible contact number, city, reason, at least one interest, adult declaration and privacy acknowledgement. Missing or implausible entries remain unaccepted. Browser validation is not identity verification and cannot stop a manually constructed POST.
2. Exclude every `SPHERES QA`, test, spam and invalid record from counts. Controlled verification records are marked as spam after testing. Do not send invitations to the reserved test numbers or example.com email addresses.
3. Normalise WhatsApp numbers before comparing them: remove spaces, brackets and dashes; convert Zimbabwe local `07…` to `+2637…`; convert `00` prefixes to `+`; preserve a supplied international country code. Do not infer a foreign country code. Compare normalised phone first and lower-case email second. Shared phone numbers need human review rather than automatic rejection.
4. Keep a private register with the Netlify submission ID, normalised contact, city, interests, main reason, consent choices, review date and status. Use `pending`, `accepted`, `duplicate`, `invalid`, `test` and `withdrawn`. Record `duplicate_of` for repeated entries. Do not copy optional preferences into general invitation lists.
5. A founding member becomes `accepted` only after an operator reviews a complete, unique adult registration and the applicant confirms control of the supplied contact through a reply to a direct welcome message. A raw form count is not the genuine-member count. No identity documents are required for this MVP.
6. The welcome message should say that membership is free, the first community starts in Harare, and gathering details will be sent when confirmed. It must not promise dates, matches, introductions or event availability.

## Export and protect records

In Netlify, select Forms, open a form and choose **Download as CSV**. Export verified entries when preparing the weekly register. Store exports on an encrypted device or restricted operator storage, never in this public GitHub repository, the deployed website, a public spreadsheet or an open sharing link. Import phone numbers as text and disable spreadsheet formula evaluation for untrusted CSV values.

Retain one current working register. Delete redundant downloads after reconciliation. Before launch, the legal operator must approve a definite retention schedule for pending, inactive, withdrawn and privacy-request records. The current website's general retention wording is not that schedule. Erasure must cover Netlify entries, exported copies and notification emails as applicable; deleting one source does not remove the others. Record a minimal completion log without retaining the deleted profile.

## Privacy requests and consent

Review `privacy-requests` and its spam folder each working day. Record the request date, type, responsible person and response. Verify control of an existing contact channel proportionately; do not ask for identity documents by default. Correct or delete the applicable records and copies, or remove optional preferences and mark their consent withdrawn. Retain membership only if the person still wants it. Stop the corresponding messages promptly and reply once the action is complete. The operator's response deadlines and any legally required retention must be settled before launch.

Use interests and city to choose relevant membership invitations. Blank or absent `partner_offers_opt_in` means **no promotional offers**. Optional personal preferences may be used only where `sensitive_consent` is `Yes`; blank values mean no information supplied, not an inferred preference. A withdrawal overrides an older `Yes`. Do not share members' phone numbers, email addresses or optional preferences with other members or partners without specific permission. WhatsApp groups expose numbers: obtain permission before adding anyone; prefer individual messages for invitations.

## Weekly count and acquisition

Once per week, reconcile the register against both Forms folders. Count accepted unique members separately from pending registrations. Exclude tests, duplicates, invalid records and withdrawals. Summarise accepted members by city, interest, `utm_source`, `utm_campaign` and `referrer_code`; use `unattributed` for blank acquisition tags. Interests can overlap, so their totals can exceed the member count. Add weekly new accepted members and total active accepted members. Keep contact details out of the scorecard.

The current **Legacy Free** site allowance is **100 submissions per month across both forms**, including legitimate privacy requests and possible duplicates/tests. The dashboard may update daily; deleting a record does not establish that quota was restored. Check usage before each campaign. Use 50 genuine members as the first checkpoint and review capacity before approaching 75 total submissions. Before seeking 100 members in one billing period, the owner must settle capacity with Netlify or change plan deliberately. Do not automatically upgrade billing or assume the newer credit-plan form allowance applies to this legacy team.

## Public promotion

Once release blockers are closed, secure the Facebook Page and matching Instagram/TikTok accounts, use the existing rings logo and brand colours, and link bios with `utm_source=facebook`, `instagram` or `tiktok`, plus `utm_campaign=founding-members`. Keep the base website URL unchanged. A first invitation should explain the Zimbabwe community, adults 18+, free registration and that the first gatherings are still being shaped. Do not use invented attendance figures, testimonials or events.
