GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might ask.

Is it really free to join?+

Yes. Registering as a community member is free. Some future gatherings may have an event ticket price, and optional premium experiences may be introduced later.

Do I have to attend every event?+

Not at all. Choose what suits your interests, schedule and budget. There is no attendance obligation.

Is Spheres for business people only?+

No. Spheres is for adults who want to widen their social circles, connect professionally or try new experiences. You don't need a job title, business or specific income to belong.

Is Spheres a dating service?+

No. We are a broader connections community. We bring adults together through shared interests. We do not offer a matching service or share personal preferences with other members without permission.

Where will events take place?+

We're starting in Harare and will consider other cities as the community grows. There are no events on sale yet. Founding members will hear about the first gatherings when details are confirmed.
